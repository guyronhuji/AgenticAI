Jumper et al. (2021), Highly accurate protein structure prediction with AlphaFold
AlphaFold predicts the three-dimensional structure of a protein from its amino-acid sequence with accuracy competitive with experiment for a large fraction of targets. In the CASP14 blind assessment it achieved a median backbone accuracy of 0.96 Å r.m.s.d.95 (Cα root-mean-square deviation at 95% residue coverage), compared with 2.8 Å for the next best method, and a median all-atom accuracy of 1.5 Å.
The network combines an Evoformer trunk, which jointly reasons over a multiple sequence alignment representation and a pairwise residue representation using attention, with a structure module that produces atomic coordinates directly through an equivariant treatment of residue frames. Predictions come with a per-residue confidence estimate (pLDDT) that is well calibrated against real accuracy.
