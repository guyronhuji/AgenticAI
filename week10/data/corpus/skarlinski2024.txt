Skarlinski et al. (2024), Language agents achieve superhuman synthesis of scientific knowledge
PaperQA2 is a retrieval-augmented language agent for scientific literature that searches, gathers evidence from full-text papers, re-ranks and summarizes it, and only then answers, citing its sources. The agent decides for itself when to search again, when to look up citations and when it has enough evidence, rather than running a fixed retrieve-then-generate pipeline.
Evaluated with LitQA2, a benchmark of questions whose answers are in the scientific literature, PaperQA2 matched or exceeded the precision and recall of PhD-level and postdoctoral researchers on retrieval and answering tasks. Wikipedia-style summary articles written by PaperQA2 were judged more accurate than existing human-written Wikipedia articles on the same topics, and the system found contradictions in the literature at a rate that blinded expert review largely confirmed.
