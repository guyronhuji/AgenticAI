Shinn et al. (2023), Reflexion: Language Agents with Verbal Reinforcement Learning
Reflexion is a framework in which a language agent improves across attempts not by updating weights but by writing verbal reflections on feedback from the environment, storing them in an episodic memory, and conditioning the next attempt on them. The feedback can be scalar (a reward) or free-form (a failed unit test, an error message), and it is converted into a textual summary of what went wrong.
On the HumanEval code-generation benchmark a Reflexion agent reached 91% pass@1, compared with 80% for the previous state of the art, GPT-4 without reflection. The method also improved decision making in ALFWorld and reasoning in HotpotQA. The key requirement is an external evaluation signal; reflections generated without one carry little information.
