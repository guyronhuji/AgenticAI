Wei et al. (2022), Chain-of-Thought Prompting Elicits Reasoning in Large Language Models
Chain-of-thought prompting provides a few exemplars in which the answer is preceded by intermediate natural-language reasoning steps. Large models then generate their own reasoning chains before answering, which substantially improves performance on arithmetic, commonsense and symbolic reasoning tasks. The effect is an emergent property of scale: small models do not benefit and can get worse.
With only eight chain-of-thought exemplars, the 540-billion-parameter PaLM model reached state-of-the-art accuracy on the GSM8K benchmark of math word problems, surpassing a fine-tuned GPT-3 model equipped with a verifier. The paper argues that the gains come from decomposing multi-step problems into intermediate steps that the model can compute sequentially.
