Hu et al. (2021), LoRA: Low-Rank Adaptation of Large Language Models
Low-Rank Adaptation freezes the pre-trained weights of a language model and injects trainable rank-decomposition matrices into each layer of the Transformer: a weight update W0 + BA where B and A have a small inner dimension r. Only A and B are trained, so the number of trainable parameters and the optimizer memory shrink dramatically, and the learned update can be merged into the frozen weights at inference time so there is no added latency.
Applied to GPT-3 175B, LoRA reduces the number of trainable parameters by 10,000 times and the GPU memory requirement by 3 times compared with full fine-tuning with Adam, while matching or exceeding fine-tuning quality on RoBERTa, DeBERTa, GPT-2 and GPT-3. The paper also investigates the rank deficiency of adaptation updates, which explains why very small r suffices.
